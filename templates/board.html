How a signal gets on this list
1 · Fingerprint

Momentum, trend, volatility, drawdown, volume, candlestick pressure, and the shape of the last month become one description of today.

2 · Find the twins

Up to twenty years of history is searched for independent look-alike setups, then their real next month is replayed.

3 · Audit the method

The same matcher is replayed on untouched past dates. A poor audit downgrades the signal instead of being hidden.

4 · Charge for risk

Adverse movement, wide uncertainty, and headlines that fight the historical lean all subtract from the score.

Playbook ranks historical evidence, not guaranteed returns. A signal is a distribution of past outcomes, not a promise. Earnings, policy, liquidity shocks, and unprecedented events can invalidate every analog. This is research, not financial advice.
